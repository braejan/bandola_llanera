# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

Qwik 1.x + Qwik City, static site generation, Spanish content. Deployment target to be confirmed (Cloudflare Pages / Netlify / Vercel / other).

## Users

Primary users are people learning to play the bandola llanera — beginners who may have little or no prior music theory, and intermediate learners returning to formalize foundations. They arrive looking for a clear reference they can study at their own pace.

## Product Purpose

Make it possible to learn the foundations of the bandola llanera — tuning, scales (major, minor, harmonic), and basic chords — through a guided student path. Success means a beginner can land on the site and immediately see the diapason of the bandola, change the scale mode, and follow a clear next step in their learning.

## Positioning

A didactic web companion anchored to the bandola llanera as the ancestral llanero instrument of Colombia and Venezuela. Meaningfully different from static reference pages: the diapason is interactive, the scale (major / minor / harmonic) is switchable on demand, and the student is walked through the foundations instead of left to browse.

## Operating Context

Self-paced study at home or during a lesson. Used from a phone, tablet, or desktop. No accounts, no login, no social features today. Learners may want to print chord and scale charts for offline practice.

## Capabilities and Constraints

Confirmed capabilities:
- Landing page that presents the diapason of the bandola (strings and tuning visible from the first viewport).
- Interactive control to switch the displayed scale between major, minor, and harmonic.
- A guided student path that orders the foundations (tuning → scale → chords) for a beginner.

Constraints:
- Spanish copy throughout.
- Static site generation; no server runtime required for the read path.
- No fabricated evidence: no invented photos, audio, testimonials, customers, benchmarks, or pricing.
- No external user accounts in the current scope.

Open decisions:
- Concrete deploy target (Cloudflare Pages, Netlify, Vercel, or other) — to be confirmed.
- Whether the guided path is presented as a curriculum/roadmap page, a progressive in-app reveal, or both.
- Whether the site ships bilingual (es / en) or remains Spanish-only.

## Brand Commitments

- Language: Spanish.
- Origin: the bandola llanera is an instrument of the folclore llanero of Colombia and Venezuela; tone and copy reflect that origin.
- Respect for the ancestral and traditional nature of the instrument, without becoming a museum-piece voice.
- Beginner-friendly learning UX: forgiving, didactic, progressive.

## Evidence on Hand

None real on hand yet. No real photographs, recordings, charts, or testimonials exist in the repository. Future work must not fabricate any. Anything not yet sourced must be marked as a placeholder, not presented as fact.

## Product Principles

1. Concretely over abstractly — show the instrument on the very first viewport; do not lead with prose about it.
2. Didactic over flashy — guide the learner step by step; reduce, do not amplify, cognitive load.
3. Truthful over decorative — never invent evidence; mark missing material as placeholder.
4. Respectful of tradition — honor the Llanos origin in tone and copy without turning it into a costume.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Beginner learners may have no prior music theory; the UI must be forgiving and progressive, with plain Spanish and minimal jargon until each term is introduced. Open: whether a bilingual (es / en) variant is needed.
